Rule of Thumb:

  • Large unit to small unit (e.g. km → m) → Multiply (×)
  • Small unit to large unit (e.g. cm → m) → Divide (÷)
Example: 250 cm → m
Since 100 cm = 1 m, divide by 100.
250 ÷ 100 = 2.5 m
⚖️ Mass
1000 g = 1 kg
Fraction Example:
500 g → kg
500 ÷ 1000 = 0.5 kg = ½ kg
1250 g → kg
1250 ÷ 1000 = 1.25 kg = 1 ¼ kg
🥤 Volume of Liquid
1000 mL = 1 L
Decimal Example: 3 L 45 mL → L
45 ÷ 1000 = 0.045 L
3 L + 0.045 L = 3.045 L
